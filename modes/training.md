# Mode: training - Training Evaluation

For each course / certification the candidate asks about, evaluate 6 dimensions:

| Dimension | What it measures |
|-----------|------------------|
| North Star alignment | Does it bring the candidate closer to or farther from the target? |
| Recruiter signal | What do HMs think when they see this on a CV? |
| Time and effort | Weeks x hours / week. |
| Opportunity cost | What can the candidate NOT do during that time? |
| Risks | Outdated content? Weak brand? Too basic? |
| Portfolio deliverable | Does it produce a demonstrable artefact? |

## Verdicts

- **DO**: 4-12 week plan with weekly deliverables and a scoreboard.
- **SKIP**: better alternative with justification.
- **DO WITH A TIMEBOX** (max X weeks): condensed plan, essentials only.

## Priority

Training that builds credibility in "production-grade AI":
1. Evals and LLM testing.
2. Observability and monitoring.
3. Cost / reliability trade-offs.
4. AI governance and safety.
5. Enterprise AI architecture.
